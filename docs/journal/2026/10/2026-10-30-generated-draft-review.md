# Common generated draft controller and review

Added a generic common owner controller and compact review panel consuming Core's
generated-draft source/request/service/workspace contract. Caller-selected image
bytes or exact text feed explicit readiness, preparation and generation actions.
Generated products retain raw evidence and require human accept/edit/clear/discard
intent; originals and manual commentary remain independent.

The controller keeps owned work busy through inference and cancellation cleanup,
fences source/context/admission changes, and retains late terminal evidence on the
departing owner. Detach awaits owned settlement without publishing into a successor.
The borrowed service and controller have separate lifetimes. Panel rebinding and
dialog actions preserve original controller/render authority. Caller persistence
failure leaves local raw evidence inspectable and visibly reports an error without
a save claim.

Authored native Flutter specifications cover connected real-service/fake-provider
image/text generation, truthful readiness, explicit preparation, review decisions,
source/context retirement, cancellation cleanup, detached/disposed ownership,
listener reentrancy, clock/callback failures, stale button/dialog actions and manual
editing alongside unavailable generation. Execution, independent review and workflow
acceptance are owned by the parent; this record makes no execution or completion
claim. The task-local ignored dependency override points only at the designated
Core checkout.
